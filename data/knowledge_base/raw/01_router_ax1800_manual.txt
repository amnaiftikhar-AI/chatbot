SECTION 1: Wi-Fi and LAN
The NetLink AX1800 router supports 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi.
The office laptop should connect to the staff network named OFFICE-STAFF.

SECTION 2: DNS settings
The standard office DNS servers are 10.10.0.10 and 10.10.0.11.
If a device connects to Wi-Fi but cannot open internal hostnames, verify that it received one of these DNS addresses.

SECTION 3: Router restart
Restart the router only after checking power and network cables.
A restart can interrupt all connected users.
