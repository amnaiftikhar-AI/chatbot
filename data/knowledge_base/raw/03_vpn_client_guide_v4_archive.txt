SECTION 1: Error SC-809
For SecureConnect 4.1, error SC-809 may occur when the VPN gateway cannot be reached.
This older guide recommends reinstalling the VPN client.

SECTION 2: Version notice
This document is for SecureConnect 4.1 only.
It is archived and must not be used as the current procedure for version 5.2.
